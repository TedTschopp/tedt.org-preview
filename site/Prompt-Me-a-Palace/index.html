Watch Prompt Me a Palace.

Prompt Me a Palace

A dazzling hip-hop musical-theater creation number about prompt-driven speed, agentic sprawl, and the discipline that turns invention into infrastructure

Author: Ted Tschopp
https://preview.tedt.org/Prompt-Me-a-Palace/
Table of Contents

Prompt Me a Palace is a dazzling hip-hop musical-theater creation number that begins playful, fast, and seductive, driven by crisp percussion, bright piano, synth pulses, and sparkling digital textures. The Vibe Engineer leads with quick, clever rap verses that feel like live invention: every prompt becomes a prototype, every idea turns into motion.

The AI Ensemble sings and chants in layered, machine-like patterns, repeating phrases with hypnotic precision. Early choruses feel triumphant and almost magical, with big theatrical lift and rapid call-and-response. The point is not that the machine is evil. The point is that speed feels like magic until the thing being built starts building beyond the map.

As the song progresses, the music darkens: bass deepens, rhythms tighten, harmonies become uneasy, and the repeated AI phrases start to feel overwhelming. The final section shifts from solo brilliance to collaborative resolve, showing that AI speed needs architecture, security, data, operations, and IT to become something that lasts.

Lyrics

Act I, Song 3 - Prompt Me a Palace

Opening - AI Ensemble

Prompt me a palace.
Prompt me a door.
Prompt me a workflow
that wasn’t there before.

Say it, shape it,
spin it, ship it.
Watch the whole world
answer the script.

Verse 1 - Vibe Engineer

I got a blank screen looking like a stage in the dark.
One little sentence and it struck one spark.
Make me a form, make me a flow,
make me a way that the old tools won’t go.

Enter: the model wakes.
Enter: the city shakes.
A table, a button, a service, a screen,
a prototype blooming from a half-formed dream.

I say, “Agent, take this customer file.
Summarize the case and classify the style.
Draft me a note, prompt me the claim,
tag every record with a risk and a name.”

And look, the walls rise quick.
Look, the gears all click.
Yesterday’s backlog in a brand new skin,
and the whole room grins like, “Do that again.”

AI Ensemble

Again, again, again.
Ask it once and ask it again.
Again, again, again, again.
Build a gate and build a pen.

Chorus - Vibe Engineer and Ensemble

Prompt me a palace and stack it to the moon.
Give me by midnight what they needed by June.
Prompt me a bridge,
prompt me a key,
prompt me a future that can answer to me.

Quick like thunder, code like rain.
Turn my little thought into a moving train.
If the world says, “Wait,” I can make it run.
Prompt me a palace before morning comes.

Verse 2 - Vibe Engineer

Now give me your interface clean and bright.
Drag in the data, make the logic tight.
Give me a dashboard, give me a chart,
give me a workflow with a beating heart.

Give me agents with agents with agents below.
Little bright workers in a luminous row.
One reads mail, one writes back,
one finds gaps in the customer track.

One calls finance, one calls sales,
one drafts answers when the old path fails.
One checks tone, one checks time,
one makes rhythm out of office crime.

I’m not a wizard, I’m not a king.
I just know what to ask the machine to bring.
I don’t need a kingdom, I need relief.
I need a new road underneath my feet.

Dialogue - Business, Vibe Engineer, and IT

Business: Can it fix my intake?
Vibe Engineer: Already done.
Business: Can it make a report?
Vibe Engineer: Give me one run.
Business: Can it scale to the region?
Vibe Engineer: I can try.
IT: Who approved the connection? Who named the role?
Which source is the truth? Who owns the hall?

Vibe Engineer: Just a second, let the demo breathe.
Don’t pull at the wires while the crowd believes.
The screen looks clean and the timing is tight.
We can talk about the rest after opening night.

Chorus - Vibe Engineer and Ensemble

Prompt me a palace and take it to the moon.
Give me by midnight what they needed by June.
Prompt me a bridge,
prompt me a key,
prompt me a future that can answer to me.

Quick like thunder, code like rain.
From a little thought into a moving train.
If the world says, “Wait,” I can make it run.
Prompt me a palace before morning comes.

Breakdown - AI Ensemble

More agents, more tables,
more triggers, more labels,
more branches, more chains,
more outputs, more names.

The palace keeps growing past the edge of the map.
Every new room has a hidden trap.
This door calls a door,
that hall calls three,
and one small chain starts chasing me.

Counterpoint - Company

The model says likely.
The business says sure.
The user says faster.
The logs stay obscure.

What did it alter?
Where did it learn?
Who gave it access?
What did it return?

Vibe Engineer

I asked for a palace, it gave me a maze.
I asked for a candle, it gave me a blaze.

AI Ensemble, Accelerating

Generate, replicate, summarize, escalate,
automate, delegate, validate, validate.

Vibe Engineer

Wait, why is the agent retrying that call?
Why did the dashboard disagree with the model?
Why is the form changing fields on its own?
Why does clean code feel so unknown?

Bridge - IT, Architecture, Security, Data, and Operations

A palace needs pillars, a system needs sight,
a spark needs a landing to carry the light.
Name every doorway, trace every stream.
Own every heartbeat, frame every dream.

Vibe Engineer

Then don’t take the fire. Don’t bury the bits.
Teach me the structure. Help me make it fit.

Final Chorus - Company

Prompt us a palace, but ground it in stone.
Build it together so it isn’t alone.
Prompt us a bridge but show us the span.
Make room for the maker, the map, and the plan.

Quick like thunder, code like rain,
but give everybody their name and a chain.
If the world says, “Wait,”
we can still make it run
with more IT in it
before morning comes.

Outro - AI Ensemble and Vibe Engineer

Prompt me a palace.
Prompt me a door.
Prompt me a future
that can last more.

What happens when the thing I built
starts building, too?

About Ted

  • I’m Ted Tschopp, a Senior Enterprise Architect based in Los Angeles, working at the intersection of enterprise architecture, artificial intelligence, software strategy, and digital automation.
  • At Southern California Edison, I help shape the architecture strategy for AI, software, and enterprise products that support one of the nation’s largest electric utilities. My work sits where strategy meets execution: platforms, guardrails, operating models, governance, risk, developer experience, and the practical realities of making ambitious technology useful, resilient, and sustainable inside a large organization.
  • I’ve spent 25+ years at SCE helping teams navigate complex technology change. The part of enterprise architecture I care about most is not the diagram. It is the design discipline that makes systems usable, governable, sustainable, and welcoming for the people who depend on them and the teams who build them.
  • Before joining Edison, I owned a business featured in Wired Magazine, founded one of the largest J.R.R. Tolkien community websites, and worked in automotive market research. That mix of technology, storytelling, community-building, and analysis still shapes how I think about systems today.
  • I love working in technology because it lets me watch the future arrive in real time. Some of the things I have worked on were science fiction when I was in school. Now they are platform problems, governance problems, people problems, and architecture problems — which is where the interesting work begins.
  • Unless otherwise stated, everything here reflects my own views.
