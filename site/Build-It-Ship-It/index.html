Watch Build It. Ship It.

Build It. Ship It.

A high-energy opening number for citizen builders, AI builders, and the enterprise trying to move at full speed without coming apart

Author: Ted Tschopp
https://preview.tedt.org/Build-It-Ship-It/
Table of Contents

Build It. Ship It. is a high-energy opening number with a hip-hop musical-theater style: fast ensemble rap, punchy sung hooks, overlapping character lines, and a driving beat that feels like a company waking up at full speed. The music should start with whispered rhythmic fragments, then build into layered percussion, piano stabs, orchestral hits, bass, and digital textures.

Each group has its own vocal flavor. Citizen Builders are urgent and practical. AI Builders are quick and dazzling. IT is precise and grounded. Security is sharp. Operations is low and steady. Data is crisp. Architecture is measured. The chorus should feel big, clever, and communal, like a whole enterprise moving in rhythm.

By the end, the energy turns from excitement to tension, landing on the question: if everyone can build, who keeps it together?

Lyrics

Act I, Song 1 - Build It. Ship It.

Company, whispered, building

Build it. Ship it. Prompt it. Click it.
Drag it. Drop it. Patch it. Fix it.
Name it. Frame it. Train it. Run it.
Need a new way? Someone’s done it.

Business Leaders

Welcome to the enterprise, city in motion,
Every team with a dream and a digital notion.
Markets move quick, customers shout,
If the door won’t open, we route around out.

Citizen Builders

I got a form and a flow and a pain point burning,
I got a process loop that the old gears keep turning.
Give me a field, give me a rule, give me five minutes flat,
I can build a little bridge over all of that.

AI Builders

I got a prompt in the box and a model on call,
I can sketch up a service from a sentence on the wall.
Prototype rising, new agent alive,
Yesterday impossible, today by five.

IT Team

Hold up - who owns it? Who sees the trace?
Who grants the access? Who governs the space?
Who checks the data when the numbers divide?
Who knows what’s running on the other side?

Full Company

Build it. Ship it. Prompt it. Click it.
Drag it. Drop it. Patch it. Fix it.
Everybody’s got a key, everybody’s got a spark,
Everybody’s building cities in the corporate dark.

Operations

I hear the hum beneath the floor,
New little engines knocking at the door.
Every shortcut has a heartbeat. Every heartbeat draws heat.
Every heat wave finds me when the job’s incomplete.

Security

I see tokens with no names,
Roles with no lanes,
Doors held open by a hope and a chain.
I don’t kill motion, I keep trust lit -
You want to move fast? Then account for it.

Data Steward

I see “customer” spelled in seven different ways,
Seven little truths in seven displays.
Dashboard bright, definition thin,
Everybody wins till the audit walks in.

Architect

I see towers rising without a street,
Apps with no foundation but a very nice beat.
Temporary tools with a permanent crown,
If we don’t draw the pattern, the pattern comes down.

Citizen Builders

We are not rebels, we are not thieves,
We are the people with rolled-up sleeves.
We felt the friction, we found the seam,
We built the thing because we heard the scream.

AI Builders

We are not magic, we are not gods,
We are ten thousand possible odds.
Give us direction, give us a frame,
Or watch the fire forget its name.

Business Leaders

Revenue, service, promise, speed,
Every department has a need.
If the old road breaks and the new tools fit -
Are we wrong to build it? Are we wrong to ship it?

IT Team

No.
But bring us in before it spreads,
Before the hidden wire threads
Through every system, every queue,
Before the fix becomes the truth.

Company, overlapping

Build it! / Who owns it?
Ship it! / Who runs it?
Prompt it! / Who checks it?
Click it! / Who protects it?

Drag it! / Who maps it?
Drop it! / Who backs it?
Patch it! / Who tracks it?
Fix it! / Who makes it fit?

Lead Narrator

Look up - the skyline is learning to grow.
Look down - the understructure’s starting to glow.
Look left - somebody made a workflow sing.
Look right - somebody’s agent just touched everything.

The city is building with invisible hands,
Through conference rooms, keyboards, requests, and commands.
No single tower can see the whole view,
But every little window says, “I know what to do.”

Full Company - Chorus

Build it. Ship it. Make it move.
Find the gap and cut the groove.
Every worker, every team,
Every backlog births a dream.

Build it. Ship it. Light the grid.
Do the thing the old way hid.
But when the sparks become a storm,
Who gives the lightning shape and form?

Citizen Builder, emerging

I have a customer waiting.
I have a promise due.
I have one clean request
sitting quiet in the queue.

IT Voice

We see your ticket.

Citizen Builder

Do you see the clock?

IT Voice

We see the risk.

Citizen Builder

Do you see the block?

Business Ensemble

Need it by Monday.
Needed it last June.
Needed by sunrise.
Needed this afternoon.

Full Company, building faster

Build it. Ship it. Prompt it. Click it.
Every answer moves too quick.
Drag it. Drop it. Patch it. Fix it.
Every shortcut lays a brick.

Lead Narrator

So here’s the age, here’s the view:
The builder is many, the system is too.
The future is open, the tools are lit -
Everyone can build it. Everyone can ship it.

Full Company, sudden stop

But if everyone can build…

IT, Security, Data, Ops, Architect

Who keeps it together?

Citizen Builder, spoken into the next song

There’s a ticket in the queue.
